SolidPledge

Assurance pledges on Bitcoin Cash. Fund it together — or nobody pays at all.

  1. 1. Create a campaign — target + deadline, get a contract address & share link.
  2. 2. Supporters pledge BCH. The money locks in the contract, not in anyone's wallet.
  3. 3. Target reached? Anyone can trigger the payout to the creator. Not reached by the deadline? Every pledger refunds themself.

Chipnet only. This demo runs on the Bitcoin Cash testnet (chipnet) with free faucet coins — no real BCH moves anywhere.

How the contract works

SolidPledge is written in SolidCash (.scash, compiled with solidc) and tested with @solidcash/testing (11 mock-network tests). It is an assurance contract with two exits, enforced by covenant introspection:

  • payout() — anyone can release the funds to the creator, but only as a single output paying the creator at least the target. An unfunded campaign can never pay out.
  • refund(pubkey, sig) — after the deadline, each pledger reclaims their own UTXO to their own address. Before the deadline, no refund is possible, so a funded campaign cannot be drained early.

Both paths were proven with real chipnet transactions (see README): payout tx e2b182d3… paid the creator 6,000 sat; refund tx ea92cd20… returned 2,500 sat to the pledger. Pledges are plain P2SH payments — the campaign exists as soon as its address is computed.

Honest limits: in this sandbox the app reads the chain over HTTPS (mainnet-js REST) and signs locally with libauth, because Electrum WebSockets are blocked here. On a normal network the same Cashonize wallet can sync over Electrum directly. Chipnet blocks are ~10 minutes apart, so confirmations are slow — the app shows unconfirmed pledges immediately.
